# "Everyone mocked it" EP. 01 · Geoffrey Hinton · voice-over script

Read at an even pace, about 2.7 words a second (36 seconds). Hit the capitalised word in each sentence; the edit
puts that word on screen on the frame you say it. Leave the one pause where marked. No music under the recording.

For forty years, they called his bet a DEAD END.
In 2024, it won the NOBEL Prize.
His name is GEOFFREY HINTON.
His own supervisor told him to GIVE UP.
They said neural networks were a WASTE of time.
So he moved to Toronto.
And kept going. For TWENTY-FIVE MORE years.
Then, in 2012, two students built ONE NETWORK. It beat every rival.
Today, that idea runs nearly EVERY AI you use.
(pause, half a second)
Forty years later, he was RIGHT.
How long would you have lasted?
Your idea isn't dead. It's EARLY.
KEEP GOING. POST. LEARN. AGAIN.

(3.5 seconds of sound only: "Follow for more.")
