# Script v1 — “Figma is useful. Design is bigger.”

**Format:** 9:16 short-form reel  
**Target runtime:** about 55 seconds at a natural speaking pace  
**Language:** English only  
**Audience:** people new to design who think learning Figma alone will make them designers  
**Core point:** Figma helps you explore and communicate ideas. Design also needs UX thinking, taste, and judgment. Coding agents can help implement a clear direction, while the designer remains responsible for the choices.

## Script notes

- The hook challenges the idea that Figma alone equals design, without fear-baiting beginners or dismissing the tool.
- “Can help” keeps the coding-agent claim measured. The script does not promise that an agent will make a good product without direction or review.
- The title and labels in the storyboard are visual support, not additional spoken claims.
- No numbers, product comparisons, or time-sensitive claims are introduced.
