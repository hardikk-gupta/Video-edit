# "Only learning Figma? You're dead." · instructions for this video

## Brief (Hardik, 2026-11-04, close paraphrase)
For newcomers to design who think learning Figma is enough. The reality: Figma is now a small part, used for
brainstorming, ideation and getting a specific screen right. The rest is your creative brain, your UX brain,
understanding why everything exists, and judgment, because AI agents (Claude Code, Codex, Cursor) can take a design to
code. With taste you can shorten the design process: gather references, then go straight to your agent. Hook direction:
"if you're learning only Figma, you're dead". Write the script first, then a storyboard with imagery, using the repo's
skills, and **build it in HyperFrames** this time.

**Answers given up front:** faceless VO over a fully designed video; English, about 45 s; main hook "Only learning Figma?
You're dead."; this round delivers **script + storyboard, then stops** for approval; CTA "Comment DESIGN".

## Skills used, in order
`viral-video-writing` (facts sheet, 5 hooks, 3 drafts, `score.py` until 90+ with no hard fails) → `asset-plan`
(claims checked on the live page) → `hyperframes` (story.md: three storylines; spec.md: SPEC with numbers and banned
defaults; storyboard.md: posed stills in the real composition) → `reel-review` before anything is shown as a film.
Render-ops, sfx-design (sfx-kit) and caption-align come after approval.
