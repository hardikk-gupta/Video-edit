Only learning Figma? You're dead.

Storyline A, "The desk": a top-down cutting-mat desk where Figma becomes one small object next to taste, UX judgment and an AI agent. 12 shots, ~46 s, 1080×1920, faceless VO. Posed in HyperFrames; animation starts after your go.

Act 1: The hook
01: The flatline
0.0 to 2.2s

VO: If you're only learning Figma, you're dead.

Moves: Frame 0 = a design canvas with a red heart-monitor line already beating; tape prints ONLY LEARNING FIGMA?; the line goes flat on "dead", the red tape slaps on.

Sound: monitor beep (synth), label-maker clunk, impact-medium-soft on DEAD

02: Even Figma ships this
2.2 to 6.1s

VO: Even Figma now ships an AI that turns one prompt into a working app.

Moves: Camera pushes into the real figma.com/make capture; red pen circles the headline; the sticky note lands.

Sound: move-zoom-in, writing-marker-draw, foley-paper-touch

Act 2: The turn
03: The buttons fall off
6.1 to 8.8s

VO: So knowing the buttons is not the job anymore.

Moves: The toolbar's tools pop off the screen as physical keycaps and drop onto the mat, one squash each.

Sound: pop-medium-out x4, impact-light-hard x4

04: Figma is the sketchbook
8.8 to 12.4s

VO: Figma is your sketchbook now, for brainstorming and for nailing one specific screen.

Moves: Camera pulls up: the laptop shrinks beside a sketchbook of wireframes; one printed screen slides out (this one screen).

Sound: move-zoom-out, foley-paper-slide, writing-pencil-draw

05: The rest is you
12.4 to 14.2s

VO: The rest comes from your head.

Moves: Camera rises to an empty mat; a white paint-marker head outline draws itself around the space.

Sound: writing-marker-draw, whoosh-slow-soft

Act 3: The three skills
06: 1 · Taste
14.2 to 18.5s

VO: First, taste, so you collect the right references and you know which one feels right.

Moves: Four real reference prints are dealt onto the mat (Stripe, Apple, Linear, Vercel); one gets the red circle and a tick.

Sound: mechanical-shutter-light per print, foley-card-touch, writing-pen-tick

07: 2 · UX judgment
18.5 to 23.0s

VO: Second, UX judgment, so you know why every element exists and what the user needs next.

Moves: Push into the printed sign-up screen: paint-marker questions appear, the Phone number field gets struck through in red.

Sound: writing-pen-write, writing-scribble

08: 3 · The agent builds
23.0 to 29.3s

VO: Third, you hand it to an AI agent like Claude Code, Codex or Cursor, and it builds the real thing from your design.

Moves: The marked-up print slides into the terminal; tabs Claude Code / Codex / Cursor; lines type; the phone lights up with the built screen (no phone field).

Sound: foley-paper-slide, keyboard-typing-quick-medium, notification-success-soft

Act 4: Payoff and proof
09: Idea → product
29.3 to 33.5s

VO: So you skip the long handoff and go straight from idea to a working product.

Moves: The handoff papers (spec, redlines, ticket, QA) pile up, then get crossed out; a white arrow draws from the idea note to the phone.

Sound: props-page-turn, writing-marker-draw, whoosh-medium-soft

10: No Figma file
33.5 to 39.1s

VO: And now the proof, this video has no Figma file behind it, only references, judgment and Claude writing the code.

Moves: This project's real folder in a terminal: ls, then find *.fig → (nothing), circled in red; "0 Figma files".

Sound: keyboard-typing-quick-short, keyboard-return-soft, writing-pen-tick

Act 5: Callback and CTA
11: Train your eye
39.1 to 43.3s

VO: So learn Figma, but don't stop there, and train your eye and your UX brain.

Moves: Callback to frame 1: the same laptop, small now, the heart line beating again, surrounded by the prints, the note and the agent.

Sound: monitor beep x2 (alive), shimmer-short-soft

12: Comment DESIGN
43.3 to 46.0s

VO: Comment DESIGN and I'll send you my full setup.

Moves: COMMENT prints out on tape, DESIGN slams on in red; the sticky lands.

Sound: label-maker clunk, impact-heavy-firm, sting-minimal-short
