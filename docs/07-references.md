# 07 · References studied

References come with every brief. Log each one here with what was taken from it.

## Smart Studios: "Image Carousel 3d Animation" (Pinterest)

- Pin: https://in.pinterest.com/pin/1113515076648702892/ · 720×540, 18.5s
- **Took:** the dashed red guide grid with dots at the crossings; a layout that re-divides the frame per beat;
  heavy condensed red headlines (we used Anton); tiny uppercase meta labels in stacks of three ("EST. 2025 /
  MOTION / DESIGN / FRAME 01"); cells that turn solid red with white type; images sitting inside cells.
- Colours sampled: background `#D8D8D8`, red `#AB1D0E`.

## Wix Motion Studio: "Bass 44" (Base44) (Pinterest)

- Pin: https://in.pinterest.com/pin/311452130503859453/ · 702×396, 51.7s
- **Took:** clean off-white product UI cards (notifications, dashboards, a ring chart), circle-check lists,
  sentences with dashed-underlined keywords ("If **new subscriber** send **welcome email**"), small icons
  beside labels, counters.
- Colours sampled: background `#F3F1ED`, accent orange `#FF6A13`, lavender chips `#C9C6F5`.

## Jitter: "Showcase – Infinite Loop Motion Template" (Pinterest)

- Pin: https://in.pinterest.com/pin/248612841927098886/ · 720×900, 9s
- **Took:** the black canvas; dark glass cards; the slate pill with a heavy word ("KEEPS") over a blurred
  stage; blur-to-sharp entrances; Inter + Space Mono; frame furniture (logo top-left, mono labels across the
  top bar, mono spec list bottom-left, "YR25" bottom-right); restraint.
- Hardik's note: "see like this looks much premium the colors and everything".

## How they were merged (v3)

Grid, Anton headlines, red cells and labels from Smart Studios + black, glass, slate pills, blur and
furniture from Jitter + dashed-underlined sentences and functional UI cards from Base44. One red, one slate.
